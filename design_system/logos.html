Sizing and Usage

Ideal dimension for logo use are 160 x 160 px with a stroke weight of 6. For every 30 px above or below standard size, stroke weight should increase or decrease by 1 (eg. 190 x 190 px should have a stroke weight of 7, 130 x 130px should have a stroke weight of 5).

Please do not change colors, distort the shape and aspect ratio, or crop.
