Welcome to Prism Design System


About

Prism is my own personal design system that reflects my approach to UX design: start with clarity, then build with intention. A minimal foundation keeps things focused and easy to navigate, while accents of color highlight moments of creativity and impact. Like light passing through a prism, I believe the strongest ideas come from a clear, thoughtful process, where simplicity makes space for something more vibrant to emerge.

Foundations

The foundation of the design system includes:

  • Typography
  • Color Palette
  • Logo
